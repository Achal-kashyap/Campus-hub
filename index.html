OUR CAMPUS MISSION

DEMOCRATIZING CAMPUS ACCESS.

CampusPulse was conceived at the intersection of student ambition, design craft, and campus solidarity.

Every student deserves equal access to university opportunities.

In large university campuses, institutional information inequality is a real problem. First-generation students, commuters, and transfers often miss out on funded fellowships, club officer elections, and lab openings simply because they don't have existing social connections to pass the flyer.

CampusPulse levels the playing field by providing a single, open, beautifully designed hub where any organization can publish and any student can discover without barriers.

Our Accessibility & Inclusivity Commitments

Engineered to conform with WCAG AA accessibility standards.

High Contrast Ratios: Both Dark (Yellow on Black) and Light (Yellow on White) themes are strictly calibrated for readability.
Complete Keyboard Operability: All interactive modals, filters, tabs, and drawers feature visible focus rings and ESC handlers.
Motion Reduction: Respects prefers-reduced-motion by pausing particle spawners and 3D tilt effects automatically.
Screen Reader Accessible: Equipped with ARIA live regions, semantic landmarks, and skip links.
